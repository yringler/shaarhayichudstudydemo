## §18–20

**Just as the Essential Self of the Unlimited One (*Atzmoot Ein Sof*) is an absolutely simple light**, here the text speaks of the Source of the light (*Maor*); **so is this [the case] with His light and ray, which is called the Unlimited Light (*Ohr Ein Sof*)**. [▶ 5:44](listen:002#344)

This is **“Before the creation of the world there was Him and His Name alone.”** [▶ 6:00](listen:002#360) The plain meaning is that there is only Him and His Name: there is no other level, that is all there is. The word "alone" is a hint that is not explained here. [▶ 6:19](listen:002#379) "Him" is the *Atzmus Ein Sof* and "His Name" is the *Or Ein Sof*. [▶ 6:41](listen:002#401)

**This [level] is called the “upper purity”** (*tehiru ila'a*). *Tehiru ila'a* is everything before the *tzimtzum*: not only *Yachid*, but *Kadmon* too. [▶ 6:51](listen:002#411)

## §21–23

**This is the matter of the revelation of the light of His Essential Self, specifically as it is**: the actual revelation, exactly as He is. [▶ 7:11](listen:002#431) As explained elsewhere, this is the revelation to Himself. It is not yet the revelation that will go out to others; it is to nobody at all (*mamash* to nobody). [▶ 7:16](listen:002#436)

**This [level] is called *Yachid*-Singular. That is, there is nothing besides Him**. [▶ 7:57](listen:002#477) Yet even this *ein od milvado* (there is nothing besides Him) is the most external part of it, its actual *gilui* (revelation), which could almost be called its spreading forth. [▶ 8:07](listen:002#487) One cannot even call it a unification or a unity; the terms do not apply. Even to call it *Yachid* is wrong, since *Yachid* is itself a word of unity. [▶ 8:23](listen:002#503)

**This is the meaning of, “You are one, but not in enumeration” of ten.** You are one, and not with a reckoning. Which reckoning? Ten: the ten *sefiros*. [▶ 8:49](listen:002#529)

## §24–25

Why then do they say **“He is wise, but not with a knowable wisdom”**, implying **various categories and levels [such as] wise, understanding, merciful**? [▶ 9:10](listen:002#550) Calling Him wise does not describe Him. Since He made people with *chachma* and *bina*, one has to say that He is wise. [▶ 9:29](listen:002#569)

When one says of a person that he has no *chachma*, it means he is stupid. When one says there is no *chachma* by the *Aibershter*, it does not mean a lack of *chachma*; it means "not with a knowable wisdom": it is not like our *chachma*, but so far beyond *chachma* that the word cannot be used there. [▶ 10:02](listen:002#602) More than that, the whole matter of *chachma* comes from Him. *Chachma* does not define Him; He defines *chachma*, and it gets everything from Him, the opposite of a wise man, whom his wisdom defines. [▶ 10:44](listen:002#644)

And *chachma* includes its opposite. *Taanug* (delight) is really the power to have pleasure or its opposite: the ability to feel pain also comes from the inner *Keser*, from *Atik*. In the same way the ability to be stupid comes from Him, so one would also have to say He is "stupid, but not with a knowable stupidity": a *shtus d'kedusha*, a holy foolishness. [▶ 11:44](listen:002#704) There has to be a place for foolishness too; otherwise there could be no foolish person. [▶ 13:05](listen:002#785)

## §26–28

The parenthesis about the *Mikdash Melech* quoting the *Arizal* is a reference, like a footnote. [▶ 13:46](listen:002#826)

**This is also the meaning of the first general teaching of the *Zohar*, “In the beginning of the rule of the King, He engraved an engraving in the Upper Purity.”** [▶ 14:02](listen:002#842) **“He engraved an engraving,” which is the matter of engraving letters**: He engraved letters. As will be learned later, there is the simple will (*ratzon hapashut*), and within it the absolute will (*ratzon hamuchlat*); that is the engraving of letters. [▶ 14:22](listen:002#862)

## §29–31

**“The beginning of the rule [of the King],” refers to when it arose in His simple desire. This is the aspect of *Keter***: He wants a certain thing. **The aspect of the letters which He engraved is the aspect of *Malchut***. [▶ 14:39](listen:002#879)

**If this is the case, then there is a beginning and end, a *Keter* and *Malchut***, in the Essence itself. And more: there must be a *chachma*, for how did He figure out how to make letters? A dog can have a will and cannot write it. There must be a *bina*, since dividing into letters needs *bina*; and if there is *bina*, presumably there are *middos*. [▶ 14:53](listen:002#893)

So what is going on? First the text said there is no *Keser* and *Malchus*, now it says there is; and it has already done this several times, saying there is and there isn't. [▶ 15:25](listen:002#925) **The explanation of this is that it arose in His simple thought and desire, “*Ana Emloch –* I shall rule.”** [▶ 15:43](listen:002#943)

## §32

On *Ana Emloch* one may add: with whom did He consult? With the souls of the righteous (*tzaddikim*). [▶ 15:48](listen:002#948) *Emloch* means "I will rule" and *nimlach* means "consult"; it is basically the same word. So one can ask through whom He became King when He said *Ana Emloch*. It is the souls of the *tzaddikim*, and "Your people are all *tzaddikim*." So the source of a Jew is higher than the primordial thought (*machshava kduma*): it is in the *Ana Emloch*, and really even above it, since they gave the *Ana Emloch* its existence. [▶ 16:09](listen:002#969)

**All this is still totally within His Essential Self**. [▶ 17:23](listen:002#1043) It is the engraving (*glif glifu*), and the engraving has levels: one in *Yachid*, one in *Echad* and one in *Kadmon*. The *shiur* (estimation) is in *Kadmon*. Usually the engraving is identified with the *shiur*, but here the text speaks of an engraving higher than the *shiur*: it is in the simple will, in *Yachid*. So the actual decision for the Jew is in *Yachid*. [▶ 17:44](listen:002#1064) And since *Yachid* is the revelation of the Essence exactly as it is, it comes from the Essence: it is the *Aibershter* who wants the Jew. Otherwise the Jew could not exist, for He is the place of everything, and the light could not do something He would not give a place to. [▶ 18:35](listen:002#1115)

The *shiur*, by contrast, is what comes **after the first *Tzimtzum,* which is called the “empty place”**. [▶ 19:36](listen:002#1176) This raises the question: on one side the Essence is a perfect unity with no parts, and on the other it speaks of parts. [▶ 19:55](listen:002#1195)
