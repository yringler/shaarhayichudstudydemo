## §33–34

**The analogy for this** is **the powers of the [human] soul, as they come into revelation from desire until action**. [▶ 20:06](listen:002#1206) **All these [powers] and their specifics exist in the concealed light of the essence of the soul, which is called the aspect of *Yechidah***. The *yechida* is one undivided thing and is not made up of powers, but one still has to say that the powers are there. [▶ 20:10](listen:002#1210)

## §35–36

Take **a person [who] desires kindness and goodness**: he is a kind person in himself. [▶ 20:28](listen:002#1228) One might object: his self is just his self, it is not made up of *middos*. True; but it is not that he is just himself with various add-ons. He himself is kind, so one has to say that kindness is there as a particular, even though it is not a separate thing. [▶ 20:46](listen:002#1246)

**It is because of this that this desire for kindness comes into revelation from its concealment**: that is why one sees open kindness. [▶ 21:26](listen:002#1286)

> **Mashal:** The *schnorrers* tell each other where to go: "Go to him, he's a good guy, he'll help you." How do you know? Do you know the future? "I don't need to know the future. He helped me, he helped Shloimy, he helped Chaimy, so he must be a good guy." Not that he *has* a kindness, which he might lose one day if he got hit on the head; he himself is good. That is why the *chafetz chesed* (desire for kindness) comes out of hiding and he helps one person after another. [▶ 21:32](listen:002#1292)

## §37–38

So **we must say, with certainty, that in the essence of his soul, this desire for kindness exists in a way of an essential *Heyulie* power** (*hiyuli*: raw potential). [▶ 22:53](listen:002#1373) What did he do for you? Shoes. For Chaimy? A coat. For Shloimy, a sandwich. "But I need pants. Do you know anybody he gave pants to?" No, but he has a kindness, so he will give you pants. How can one know, if he never gave pants to anybody? Because the *chafetz chesed* is *hiyuli*. [▶ 23:02](listen:002#1382) **It includes within itself everything that he may come to desire of kindness and goodness, in every particular specific that may be asked of him**: pants too are included in the *hiyuli*. [▶ 24:01](listen:002#1441)

Yet it is not made up of those particulars. One cannot say a person's essential kindness consists of wishes to give sandwiches, shoes and clothing; what it is, is kindness. But if one particular is missing, it is not kindness. [▶ 5:02](listen:003#302) This is what the *Pasach Eliyahu* means: whoever separates one of the ten *sefiros* from the others, it is as if he separated You. The Essence is not made of the *sefiros*, but take one out and it is no longer the *Aibershter*; he is a *kofer* (denier) in the whole. [▶ 5:45](listen:003#345) Some say "G-d is love," unconditional love; but that takes out the *midda* of *din* (judgement), which says there are conditions. And someone who will do anything for you except give shoes, so that you walk around without shoes: that is not kindness anymore. [▶ 6:23](listen:003#383)

## §39–40

**Prior to his revealed arousal of desire and will to express his kindness in this particular deed, it was included in the essential *Heyulie* desire for kindness**: a kindness for pants was included, for otherwise how could it come out? [▶ 24:15](listen:002#1455) [▶ 7:21](listen:003#441) On the other hand, the essential kindness is not made up of separate kindnesses for pants, coats and sandwiches. [▶ 24:51](listen:002#1491)

In the *mashal* the sandwich itself is not in the kindness: the man has a kindness for sandwiches, but the sandwich is external, and he cannot keep giving them out unless he is rich. In the *nimshal* (what the analogy stands for) the actual sandwich comes from there. [▶ 25:14](listen:002#1514) People tend to make it more and more abstract. But we are told to say a blessing on the sandwich because the *Aibershter* gave it. Why thank Him for an idea of a sandwich? Thank whoever gave me the physical sandwich that filled my stomach. The blessing before and after shows that the actual sandwich came from there. In a way it is foolish to say the *Aibershter* has sandwiches, and in a way it is foolish to say the opposite: if He did not have them, where do they come from? [▶ 25:58](listen:002#1558) So the essential kindness is a *hiyuli* power that includes every particular that will be asked of it, and **it then came out of the *Heyulie* state to become revealed in this particular act**. [▶ 7:37](listen:003#457)

## §41–42

**We observe that in the [revealed] desire for kindness, there is intellect, wisdom, emotions, thought, speech and action**. [▶ 7:52](listen:003#472) When the kindness comes into actuality, there is *sechel* (intellect) in it: one has to figure out exactly how to do it. A man says his family is starving, but he is a drunk; give him money and he will spend it on liquor and come home and beat his family. So one sends food to his wife instead. [▶ 8:12](listen:003#492) Then there are *middos*: he has to feel kindness and pity in his heart. Then *machshava* (thought), "Okay, let's do this"; *dibur* (speech), "Okay, I'll help you"; and *maaseh* (action). So there are ten *sefiros*: *sechel* is *chachma*, then all the *middos*, and thought, speech and action are *Malchus*. [▶ 8:59](listen:003#539)

**It therefore is certain that in the source of this desire for kindness, while it was concealed in the essential *Heyulie* desire for kindness, there too, it included these divisions**; otherwise how could it come out so? The revealed kindness has ten *sefiros*, so the hidden kindness must have them too. [▶ 9:34](listen:003#574)

The same point from the other side: if the kindness for sandwiches were not included in the *hiyuli* kindness, it would not be a kindness at all. [▶ 28:21](listen:002#1701) There are foundations whose charter is to give shoes. A man can drop dead of starvation on their floor and they will not give him a sandwich. Perhaps it was started by a kind man who would have given a sandwich too, but now the foundation gives shoes. Is that kindness? No, it is not kindness anymore. [▶ 28:46](listen:002#1726) It is a bureaucracy. That is the whole matter of the world of *Tohu*: the original thing got separated from the Essence, so it is no longer kindness but a kindness of *Tohu*. [▶ 1:20](listen:003#80)

## §43

**However, in the aspect of the essential *Heyulie* desire for kindness, as it exists prior to being expressed into a particular thing**, though the divisions are there too, **they are included and unified in an ultimate state**. [▶ 10:02](listen:003#602) As with the shoes and sandwiches: there were not two kindnesses joined to build a bigger kindness; there was only one thing. So too the ten *sefiros* of the essential kindness. The text will later say the same of the essential kindness in the Essence of the *Ein Sof*. [▶ 10:18](listen:003#618)

> **Key point:** The kindness in a person's *yechida* is no more separate than the shoes and the sandwiches are separate within it, or than its own ten *sefiros* are; and that kindness is not separate from the other *sefiros*, nor from the Essence. [▶ 10:54](listen:003#654)
