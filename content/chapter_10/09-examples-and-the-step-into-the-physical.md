## §81

**In the aforementioned allegory of the power of movement, [this can be seen] when it is prepared and destined to come into revelation in a particular [movement]**: like grabbing the bottle. Even when he does make the movement, it is connected to this third level. [▶ 10:40](listen:004#640)

What happens when the other man grabs the bottle and the power of movement does not come into action? He says, "When you're finished, pass the bottle over." The same power of movement, which in the essence of the soul was just his wish for a drink, turned into the power of speech (*koach hadibur*). It is not a separate thing; it is the very same motivation. Already in *Kadmon* one can speak of powers unified as one. [▶ 11:00](listen:004#660)

> **Key point:** Two things are learned from this. All is prepared, and yet it need not happen; and even when it does happen, the *hiyuli* stays *hiyuli*. And one power can turn into another. [▶ 12:13](listen:004#733)

## §85

**Similarly, [regarding the example of the influx of life force], there is the estimation of the revelation and spreading forth of the spirit of life**: how much life will be revealed, prepared for what it will **enliven [the body] in actuality**. [▶ 12:42](listen:004#762) For example, when a man is psyching himself up to run, he needs an extra influx of life; before a heavy job he has to "liven up," as soldiers are told: "Liven up, we've got a big job ahead of us." [▶ 12:59](listen:004#779)

## §86

**Another example is the power of growth, as it is prepared to grow in actuality**: the power of growth in the ground, already connected to a tree and ready to give it the power to grow. [▶ 13:31](listen:004#811) **Here too, there are these three levels**. Each of these powers has the three levels, and they are all connected even to the actual deed: the deed is connected to *Kadmon*, *Kadmon* to *Echad* and *Echad* to *Yachid*. [▶ 13:55](listen:004#835) When a man grabs for the bottle, he is really grabbing it with *Yachid*, with the essence of the *yechida*, through the two channels of *Echad* and *Kadmon*: a point where the powers come out of the essence in particular, and a point where they come out for this particular action, measured exactly. [▶ 14:13](listen:004#853)

## §87–90

**[All three of these levels] are before anything came out into actuality**. This is not the thing itself coming out into action; even when it does come out, this is the inner power, which does not itself come into the action. [▶ 14:41](listen:004#881)

When it actually becomes a movement, **there is a change from a spiritual existence to a physical existence**: the movement is no longer something of the soul; it is a physical movement, and a physical life. [▶ 14:59](listen:004#899) **The same is true in the example of physical growth**: the actual plant is not the power of growth at all. It is a different thing, a plant, like a piece of wood. [▶ 15:25](listen:004#925)

## §91

**For this [change of existence] to be, it is necessary for there to be an initial *Tzimtzum* - contraction and restraint in the revelation of this spiritual**, so that it can pass **from spiritual to physical**. [▶ 15:36](listen:004#936)

> **Mashal:** In an Abbott and Costello film with the Wolf Man, Costello, seeing the Wolf Man behind Abbott, cannot get his words out. He is so panicked that he cannot contract his speech into particular letters; he wants to say the whole speech inside him at one shot, and since he cannot make the *tzimtzum*, he cannot say anything in particular. So too when a Tyrannosaurus Rex looms up over someone, people freeze, though they should be running: the shock is so great that the whole power of movement comes out at once, trying to run every way at once, and since it cannot be contracted to a particular movement it cannot run any way at all. [▶ 15:47](listen:004#947)

The spiritual power is infinite; it is not for any particular movement, and the power of speech is not for any particular thing, so it has to be contracted all the way down to the letters. That is also why people stutter: they try to put the whole thought into speech at once, and what one has to teach them is to slow down and make a *tzimtzum*. [▶ 17:43](listen:004#1063) This will be explained in chapter 12. [▶ 18:18](listen:004#1098)

Summing up the chapter: in some places it is said that there is nothing in the *Or Ein Sof*, no reckoning of ten *sefiros*, none of these *middos* at all; in others, that He engraved letters in the upper purity, that He is wise but not with a knowable wisdom, meaning there is a *chachma* there. [▶ 18:49](listen:004#1129) The explanation is that "no *sefiros*" does not mean they are lacking there, but that it is totally above the matter of *sefiros*: one can no more say there is an actual *chachma* there than that there is a will for shoes in a kindness. Yet one cannot take them out. To say He has no *chachma* at all is an insult, as if to say He were foolish; to separate any one *sefira*, even *netzach* of *tiferes*, is to deny the essence (*kofer b'ikar*), just as one who will not "do shoes" for a man with bleeding feet has no kindness, whatever else he does. [▶ 19:26](listen:004#1166) The basic point of it all: if not, where would these particulars come from? [▶ 21:44](listen:004#1304)
