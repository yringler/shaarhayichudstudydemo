## §6–7

Before starting on the particulars of the worlds, there is a certain thing to begin with. [▶ 1:20](listen:002#80) **All those who contemplate should first contemplate upon the Primal Source, Who is called, “The Source of everything”** (*Mekora d'Kula*): **the Essential Self of the Unlimited Light** (*Atzmus Or Ein Sof*), as it is before the *tzimtzum* that is called the empty place (*makom panui*). [▶ 1:35](listen:002#95)

## §8–9

**There too** the contemplation is to be **specifically in a manner of particulars**. [▶ 1:46](listen:002#106) This is the inner meaning of *ma'arich b'echad*, lengthening the word *Echad* when saying *Hashem Elokeinu Hashem Echad*: one looks at length, in detail, **specifically upon the Simple Unity**. [▶ 1:53](listen:002#113)

## §10–11

**In general, this [Simple Unity] is divided into three levels**. [▶ 2:09](listen:002#129) "In general," because it really divides into many more levels: these three are only the levels of the inner aspect of the light (*pnimiyus ha'or*), not even of the Source of the light itself (the *Maor*), and after them comes the spreading forth of the light (*hispashtus ha'or*). What is discussed here is the essence of the light, the *Atzmus Or Ein Sof*. [▶ 2:18](listen:002#138) **Each of which includes ten *sefirot***. [▶ 2:57](listen:002#177)

The three levels are *Yachid*, *Echad* and *Kadmon*, and **all this is still within the aspect of the Essential Self of the Unlimited Light** before the first *tzimtzum*: it is all still inside the Essence. [▶ 3:03](listen:002#183)

## §12–14

**The aspect of *Yachid*-Singular, connotes that He is literally alone**. [▶ 3:18](listen:002#198) **This is higher than the unity of parts, which is called *Echad*-One**. [▶ 3:22](listen:002#202) **Accordingly**, at the level of the Essence itself, **it is not yet fitting to discuss the matter of the ten *sefirot* at all**. [▶ 3:29](listen:002#209)

## §15–17

To speak of ***Malchut* of the Unlimited One** would already imply ***Keter* and *Chochmah***. [▶ 3:38](listen:002#218) *Malchus* means speech, and wherever there is speech there has to be a will (*ratzon*) and a brain before it: there is no speech without them. [▶ 3:44](listen:002#224)

**Now, in truth, even the aspect of the Essential Self of the Unlimited Light, which is called “Alone,” includes ten *sefirot* within itself**. [▶ 4:00](listen:002#240) The *Emek HaMelech* also speaks of levels of *Yachid*; what the book calls "alone" here is the most external level of *Yachid*, which is the innermost level of the *Or Ein Sof*. [▶ 4:12](listen:002#252)

**However, they are called, “He is wise, but not with a knowable wisdom”**: not with a discernible *chachma*. [▶ 4:51](listen:002#291) It can be explained in different ways, but the main explanation is that He is wise, but not with a wisdom that can be separated out so that one can point to it and say "this is the wisdom." [▶ 5:06](listen:002#306) And further, **“He is not of any of these qualities at all”**: just the opposite, the *middos* come from Him; they do not describe Him in any way. [▶ 5:24](listen:002#324)
