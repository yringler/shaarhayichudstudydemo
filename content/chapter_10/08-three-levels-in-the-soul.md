## §67–71

**We thus find that there are three levels, one following the other, that are drawn from the actual essence of the soul, which is called the *Yechidah***. [▶ 5:18](listen:004#318) **The first [level] is the ten essential *Heyulie* powers, such as the essential *Heyulie* desire for kindness**: this is *Yachid*. [▶ 5:26](listen:004#326)

**They are absolutely and totally included**: that means they actually do not exist. The *yechida* is not within their category or definition at all, **like the inclusion of the power of movement in the essential self of an animal**. [▶ 5:36](listen:004#336) So **it is not fitting to use a terminology of “unification of separate parts together”**: one cannot even call it a unification, **because they are completely included in the essential self, and it is as if they have no existence at all**. That is *Yachid*. [▶ 5:59](listen:004#359)

## §72–74

**The second [level] is the aspect of the spreading forth of the ten powers, as they are prepared to come into revelation**: *Echad*. It is already a matter of spreading forth, ready to come out in various ways, as kindness or any of the ten *sefiros*. [▶ 6:15](listen:004#375) **This is like the essential *Heyulie* desire for kindness, as it comes to do a particular act of kindness**, but **still before it is drawn out into actuality**. [▶ 6:35](listen:004#395)

> **Mashal:** *Avraham Avinu* sitting at the opening of his tent: he is ready for kindness to come out, but nothing comes out, because nobody has come yet. It is ready to come out to a particular, but there is no particular yet. That is *Echad*. [▶ 6:40](listen:004#400)

## §75–76

**Here, [on this level,] the matter of “unification” of the many particulars which are within it, is applicable**: one can say it is made of various things, [▶ 7:14](listen:004#434) **the intellect and emotions of this desire for kindness**, though even here it is not really a unification. [▶ 7:21](listen:004#441) If these powers came out of the essence, where they did not exist at all, there must be a point where they begin to have an ability to exist. That is not an actual existence, only an ability that they could exist. That is *Echad*: there one can say that this ability is made up of many abilities, all unified as one general spreading forth of the soul. [▶ 7:31](listen:004#451)

## §77

**The third [level] is when it is drawn to that particular [act of kindness], only that it has not yet come into [actuality] due to some lacking or obstacle**: *Kadmon*. [▶ 8:11](listen:004#491) This does not mean only when the act does not come about. Even when it does, this third level is connected to it; it is through the lack and the obstacle that one sees it. [▶ 8:19](listen:004#499)

> **Mashal:** At a *farbrengen* there is a bottle of vodka on the table and a man's glass is empty. As he gets ready to reach for it, the whole movement is already there in his power of movement before he makes it; that is why he does not have to grope around. He has it all worked out, where his hand will go and that it will grab the bottle. Just before he moves, someone else grabs the bottle. Nothing came out; it stayed *hiyuli*; and yet it was completely prepared. Had nobody else taken the bottle and he had grabbed it, that same preparation is what would have been connected to the outward grabbing. [▶ 8:36](listen:004#516)

So the lack is not necessarily a lack: it is how this level is seen. [▶ 10:12](listen:004#612)

## §78–80

**However, the preparation itself is already complete. The only thing that is missing is for it to come out and be revealed in actuality**. [▶ 10:18](listen:004#618) **He already estimated within himself, in a manner of particulars**, as with grabbing the bottle, **what is necessary [for him] to bring it into actuality**. [▶ 10:27](listen:004#627)
