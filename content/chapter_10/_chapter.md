## Overview

Rabbi Markel's series begins here. In its introduction he describes *Shaar HaYichud* in two parts: the first explains what *hisbonenus* is, not meditation but *iyun*, deep, repeated, analytical study of every detail of *seder hishtalshelus*; the second, from chapter 10, explains what to contemplate. [▶ 0:34](listen:001#34) [▶ 7:55](listen:001#475) The chapter starts at the top: the Essence of the *Or Ein Sof* before the *tzimtzum*, in three levels, *Yachid*, *Echad* and *Kadmon*. The ten *sefiros* are said not to be there, and yet to be there. The *mashal* is a kind person: his kindness is not made of particular kindnesses, yet without any one of them it would not be kindness. The same three levels are then traced in the soul, from powers wholly within the *yechida* to the exact estimate of one act, which needs a *tzimtzum* to become physical.
