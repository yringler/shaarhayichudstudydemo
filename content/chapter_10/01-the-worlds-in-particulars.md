## §1–2

The earlier chapters taught that *hisbonenus* (contemplation) has to be done *derech prat*, in particulars; now the book begins to explain the subject itself in that way. [▶ 0:16](listen:002#16) **We must now understand all the particulars of the four worlds of *ABY”A*** (*Atzilus*, *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*): **all the specifics in the matter of the ten *sefirot***, their lights and vessels and how they are vested one in another. This is what the rest of the book will explain, everything. [▶ 0:23](listen:002#23)

And not only in general: **all their particulars, even how each *sefirah* divides into its particular specifics**. [▶ 0:31](listen:002#31)

## §3

The example given, **the aspect of *Chessed* of *Malchut* that is in *Chochmah*, that is in *Malchut* of *Chessed***, goes five levels deep. [▶ 0:41](listen:002#41) It stands for any five levels: by the end of this study one should be able to work out any such combination, and **and the like** means it could even be six levels. [▶ 0:48](listen:002#48)

## §4–5

The book will also explain **the unifications and inclusions [of the *sefirot*] in the aspect of the *Keter* of each world**: how everything is included in the *Keser* of each world, which stands between that world and the one below it. [▶ 0:56](listen:002#56)

This rests on the known general principle that **from the aspect of *Malchut* of the upper [world], there comes about the *Keter* of the lower [world]**, all the way up. That is what will be explained from chapter 10 through the end of the book. [▶ 1:06](listen:002#66)
