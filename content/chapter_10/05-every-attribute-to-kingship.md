## §44–45

Kindness is one particular *sefira* the text gives the example on; **this likewise applies to any other particular essential qualities, such as the quality of *Gevurah*-Judgment*, Tiferet*-Beauty (or Mercy)*,* or *Netzach-*Conquest**. [▶ 11:20](listen:003#680) A man who likes to win does so because he has the *midda* of *netzach* (victory) in his soul. He wants to win all kinds of things, and to win he needs *sechel* and *middos*: even a football game is "all strategy." The strategy is the *sechel* in *netzach*; the pep talk before the game builds up the *midda*; and there is thought, speech and action in it too. [▶ 11:43](listen:003#703) One could give a *mashal* on any of the *middos*; the text gave it on kindness, perhaps because the world comes from the desire for kindness. [▶ 12:59](listen:003#779)

**[This is so,] until the quality of *Malchut-*Kingship*,* which is the quality of leadership** (*hisnasus*), which is also a *midda* the world came from. [▶ 13:24](listen:003#804) **Certainly, it too includes ten**, like all the other *sefiros*: **the desire and will for kingship** (its *Keser*), **the wisdom**, the **emotions**, and the thought, speech and action that are *Malchus* of *Malchus*. [▶ 13:41](listen:003#821)

## §46–47

**However, before it comes to an aspect of revelation in a particular thing**, the text goes back to what it said before, *Ana Emloch*, the engraving. [▶ 14:01](listen:003#841) That is why it returns specifically to *Malchus* and not the other *sefiros*. The desire for kindness was the arousal from Above that brought about having a world in the first place, but the actual making of the world was *Ana Emloch*, *Malchus*: the engraving, which is letters, which is *Malchus*. [▶ 14:12](listen:003#852) *Hisnasus*, rising above another, is what brings about the existence of another at all: "there is no king without a people." [▶ 14:45](listen:003#885)

So before it was revealed in **a particular thing, such as ruling over a specific country**, there was the original *midda* of kingship in its *hiyuli*, before anything happened: **they were absolutely included and unified in the aspect of the essential *Heyulie* desire for kingship**, **similar to** the **essential *Heyulie* desire for kindness**. [▶ 15:03](listen:003#903)
