## §65

At the start of the next tape the discussion is about sight. The eye's power to see takes in round shapes and square shapes, a sofa, a table. What about a sofa somebody has in China that you never saw and never will? The eye has the power to see that too: the power to see that particular shape exists in the eye already, and therefore in the soul. [▶ 0:32](listen:004#32) But that is the power to see the shapes, not the sofa itself. In the same way, the will for a kindness of shoes is not the shoe. That is in the *mashal*; in the *nimshal* the actual shoe comes out from it: the whole of *Atzilus*, *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*, down to the final physical thing. [▶ 1:02](listen:004#62) So "Mine is the silver and Mine is the gold" does not mean only the ability to earn money; it means the actual thing comes from there. [▶ 2:10](listen:004#130)

**We are forced to say that all these particulars that spread forth [into revelation], [exist] completely included within the essence, for if this was not so, from where did these particulars in the spreading forth of revelation come?** Where did my car come from? [▶ 2:29](listen:004#149) On one side, one cannot say there is a kindness to help a man fix his car in the essential kindness; on the other, without it the kindness is broken and is not kindness. To say my car existed inside the *Aibershter* sounds silly: He is not made up of cars. But if it did not exist in Him, He is not the *Aibershter*. Did the car come from another G-d? "But it's physical." And where did the physical come from? [▶ 2:45](listen:004#165) The *Rambam* in *Moreh Nevuchim* says that this was the mistake of the Greek philosophers: that there is G-d, and there is also a physical world. [▶ 3:40](listen:004#220) So one has to say it was there, just as all the particulars are in the essential kindness: in the *mashal* a kindness for a sandwich, in the *nimshal* the actual sandwich. [▶ 4:02](listen:004#242)

## §66

**This is similar to the light and rays that are included in the self of the sun**. [▶ 4:38](listen:004#278) When the light was inside the sun it already had all its particulars: outside in the sunlight one sees green and blue and yellow, so they must already have been inside the sun. [▶ 4:45](listen:004#285)

That ends the general explanation; now the text explains how this comes in three levels. [▶ 5:03](listen:004#303)
