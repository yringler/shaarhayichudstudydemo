## §21

**Without this power of *Tvunah* in this comprehension of *Memaleh Kol Almin*, his comprehension of it would remain in his brain of understanding alone.** There are many people studying Chassidus who have no *tevuna* in it. [▶ 19:27](listen:021#1167) **He would not know what to do with this** concept. [▶ 19:52](listen:021#1192) People ask: you learn this kind of Chassidus, but what is it good for? Will it make me money? Will it improve my *middos*? A *mussar* book improves the *middos*, but this is only an intellectual thing, good for people who like puzzles. How does it help the *davenen*? [▶ 19:56](listen:021#1196) Nor will he know how to bring this *hisbonenus* into an actual understanding of how the world comes into being, something from nothing, rather than a set of abstract explanations. [▶ 20:32](listen:021#1232)

## §22

**From the above we may [now] understand the reason for the lacking [which may be found] in beginners or even in those who are experienced, but lack *Tvunah*.** [▶ 20:52](listen:021#1252) They learned Chassidus and understood it; perhaps not every detail, but they understood. [▶ 21:01](listen:021#1261) Yet **they are incapable of bringing the matter of the coming into being of the worlds out of nothing, into contemplation during their prayers**: not necessarily in prayer, but in *hisbonenus* as it relates to the actual world. [▶ 21:15](listen:021#1275)

Because they lack *tevuna*, they also think that *hisbonenus* in *tefilla* means thinking about abstract things during the prayers, sitting and spacing out. [▶ 21:34](listen:021#1294) But the height of the whole *tefilla* is asking the *Aibershter*: make me healthy, give me a livelihood, bring us back to Jerusalem, build the Temple. *Baruch* means to draw down: bring Yourself down to here. Revive the dead; forgive us; restore the service in the Temple, and then we can all sit and learn Chassidus the whole time. [▶ 22:15](listen:021#1335) *Tefilla* means bringing G-dliness down into the world, asking that it come in actual physical things, asking the *Aibershter* to take over the world and bring Moshiach. [▶ 23:21](listen:021#1401) They do not see what contemplating how *Atik* and *Arich* are two sides of the same thing has to do with getting money. [▶ 23:52](listen:021#1432)

This concerns *avodas halev* and *tefilla*, *avodas hamochin* too, and certainly *daas Havaya*, which will be the real matter of Moshiach: not only during prayer, but in everything one does, to see that nothing happens by accident and that it is literally the *Aibershter* doing it all. One is supposed to see it, not just believe it, as with belief in *hashgacha pratis* (Divine providence). [▶ 24:15](listen:021#1455)

## §23

Because of this, **they may reach the point where they ask [themselves], “What am I to do with all this?” and “Of what benefit is this [to me].”** What is all this Chassidus good for? [▶ 24:59](listen:021#1499) There is an even higher point: Chassidus is not for the sake of money; when one asks for a livelihood, the Chassidus is the livelihood. What he wants most is the Chassidus itself, and if anything the money is for the Chassidus. [▶ 25:13](listen:021#1513)

**This is because in his mind these are two separate things**: Chassidus and the world have nothing to do with each other. The world is the world, and on Shabbos one sits with friends and does a crossword puzzle of Chassidus a little. [▶ 25:46](listen:021#1546) It is **because the understanding remains in his mind [as an abstract concept], totally removed from all matters**: he has nothing but the idea itself, and it is connected to nothing in the world or in his life. [▶ 26:22](listen:021#1582)

There are levels here. At one level it is wholly abstract and has nothing to do even with what one asks for in *tefilla*. At another it is used to bring down the light so that one will get things in the world, but it is still two things: there is G-d, and there is the world, and one asks G-d for help. That is already some *tevuna*, that something will come out of it, but it is not the real *tevuna*. [▶ 26:54](listen:021#1614) The real *tevuna* is *ein od milvado*: what one really wants is the Chassidus, and he wants health not so that the Chassidus will help his health but the opposite, so that his health and money will help his Chassidus. If we were all healthy and had money, we could all sit together and learn Chassidus all the time. It is all one thing. [▶ 27:20](listen:021#1640)
