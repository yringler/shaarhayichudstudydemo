## §1

This chapter is basically the conclusion of chapters one and two. [▶ 0:04](listen:021#4) **From all the above we understand that in the matter of contemplation (*Hitbonenut*) on the comprehension of G-dliness, there are two levels, *Binah* and *Tvunah*.** [▶ 0:18](listen:021#18) ***Binah* is the aspect of the essential comprehension of G-dliness**, studying every particular of the matter, how it is and what it is, **with a great analysis**: that is, the actual learning. [▶ 0:25](listen:021#25)

## §2–3

The example is **the comprehension of the details of *Memaleh Kol Almin*** (the light that fills all worlds), which is **the coming into being of something from nothing**, called the G-dly power in what it affects (*koach hapoel banifal*). [▶ 0:38](listen:021#38) This is the name *Adnus* or the name *Elokim*, names that mean power. [▶ 0:46](listen:021#46)

**In the externality of this matter there are many various particular facets**, until one grasps the very inside of the matter of *memalei kol almin*, how it is in its essence. **This is what was called [above], “the depth of the concept”** (*omek hamusag*). [▶ 1:27](listen:021#87)
