## §14–15

Now, **the aspect of *Da’at* is the aspect of the recognition and feeling, that is, the connection to the concept, as explained above.** [▶ 7:41](listen:021#461)

**About this it is stated**, “You shall know…” and “Know the G-d of your father…”, **which refers specifically to the comprehension of how it is that *HaShem*’s-יהו״ה G-dliness fills all worlds**. [▶ 7:48](listen:021#468) The mitzva of *daas Havaya* (knowing G-d) is specifically in the matter of *memalei kol almin*, as the verse says, “the earth shall be full of knowledge of G-d as the waters cover the sea.” [▶ 7:59](listen:021#479) So the knowledge of G-d is specifically in the *seder hishtalshelus* (chain of worlds), in *memalei kol almin*. Rabbi Markel adds that, looked at more closely, he thinks it is even more in the lower worlds (*Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*) than higher up; and the name *Havaya* is basically the four worlds, *Atzilus*, *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*. [▶ 8:18](listen:021#498) So *daas* is specifically to be connected with, to have *daas* enter, the comprehension of *memalei kol almin*. [▶ 8:59](listen:021#539)

## §16

The Yiddish in the text, “*der klepp*,” he understands as holding on, in the length of the matter. [▶ 9:13](listen:021#553) **It is specifically this that brings one to the aspect of the “depth of everything,”** that is, *hisbonenus* is what brings to it; and what is that? **the depth of *Chochmah* “which is found from nothing,”** the *taalumos chachma* (hidden depths of wisdom) explained before, which come after *hisbonenus*. [▶ 9:23](listen:021#563)

## §17–18

In other words, **the depth of the sight of the intellect**: seeing the matter with the mind. [▶ 9:46](listen:021#586) This is not the sight of prophecy, which is actually seeing with the eyes. *Re'iyas hasechel*, the sight of the intellect, means that something is explained to a person until he sees its truth: it is no longer an explanation; he actually sees the truth of it. [▶ 9:57](listen:021#597)

One might ask: the Rebbe says that even physical sight, pointing with the finger and saying “this,” comes from *hisbonenus*. Everything does come from *hisbonenus*, but what is spoken of here is the sight of the intellect, called the sight of *chachma*. [▶ 10:19](listen:021#619)

**This is as stated in *Ra’aya Mehemnah*, “with the eye of the intellect, for the heart sees all.”** With the eye of the intellect within the heart, everything is seen. **This is [also] called, “Gazing at the preciousness [of the King].”** That is, seeing the glory of the King. [▶ 10:53](listen:021#653) **Now, in this too there is an aspect of depth**: as explained before, when one reaches *chachma* there too is a length, a width and a depth, so in the sight of *chachma* too there is a depth. That completes the matter of chapter one. [▶ 11:10](listen:021#670)
