## §6–7

**The width is to expand and explain how the matter is [manifest] in many different ways, as opposed to just a single way.** That is the *rochav* (width). [▶ 2:44](listen:021#164) **For example, an analogy for this matter [may be understood] from [how] the life force of the soul [is manifested] in the body.** [▶ 2:52](listen:021#172) The life of the soul in the body is a *hispashtus min ha'etzem*, a spreading forth from the essence. [▶ 3:00](listen:021#180)

## §8

**There are a multitude of [other] ways [to understand the concept] of a spreading forth from the essence**: there are various analogies for spreading forth from the essence. [▶ 3:04](listen:021#184) So *memalei kol almin* is explained in a few ways. One is the way of spreading forth from the essence, which has a number of analogies of its own. Another way is the *gilui ha'helem*, **the revelation of that which is hidden**. [▶ 3:13](listen:021#193)

That too has various forms: **the movement of an animal, or from the letters of speech**, **insight from the essential *Chochmah***, **the revelation [of the power] of sight in the eye**, **the revelation of the flame from the coal**. [▶ 3:31](listen:021#211) Each one is a different way of saying or explaining it. **All the above are examples [of the concept] of the revelation of that which is hidden**, and all of them together are the width of one length, the revelation of the hidden. [▶ 3:50](listen:021#230)

## §9–10

**In contrast, the analogy of the ray of the sun, or the light of a candle, or an applied force of energy and the like, are analogies [for the concept] of a spreading forth** from the essence. [▶ 4:00](listen:021#240) That is a different length, and it too has various ways of being explained. [▶ 4:09](listen:021#249)

**All the above is the expansion on the essential matter**: all of it is still about the essence of *memalei kol almin*, only widened. [▶ 4:18](listen:021#258) **The opposite of this would be the short summation [and explanation of the concept] in only one way**, which is narrow, and it is also short in length, **in a short way, [only] glanced [upon] by the mind**. So there is a shortness of width and a shortness of length. [▶ 4:31](listen:021#271)
