## §19

Now he comes to chapter two. [▶ 11:29](listen:021#689) The whole exile is specifically because of a lack of *tevuna*. It says, “My people does not contemplate”: both are lacking, but more than anything it is a lack of *tevuna*. [▶ 11:38](listen:021#698)

Now, **the aspect of *Tvunah* is to draw forth from this concealed depth, into revelation**. Once one reaches the hidden depth, not only the depth of *bina* but the depth of *chachma*, where through the explanation one actually sees, in the mind, how the thing truly is, *tevuna* brings it into revelation. [▶ 11:57](listen:021#717) Here actual seeing with the eye comes in: bringing it down so that one actually sees G-dliness in the physical world, in what is actually happening. [▶ 12:32](listen:021#752) Once a person has the whole understanding, it is no longer abstract; he brings it all the way down into what he is seeing. [▶ 13:34](listen:021#814)

There is a story that the Alter Rebbe asked the Mitteler Rebbe what he had *davened* with, and he answered, with a *hisbonenus* about *Adam Kadmon*. Then he asked the Alter Rebbe what he had *davened* with, and the Alter Rebbe answered, with the *shtender*. It was not a joke: he *davened* with seeing that the *shtender* itself is *Elokus*. That is *tevuna*: to look around, apply the whole of the *bina*, and realize that one is looking at nothing but G-d, *ein od milvado* (there is nothing besides Him), and that he was looking at Him all along. [▶ 13:51](listen:021#831)

> **Mashal:** Children's magazines have a picture with ten hidden bunnies to find: among the leaves of a tree, a bunny upside down. Before finding them, one sees the whole picture and every bunny in it, but does not notice them. Once found, or once someone points them out (“here is his nose, here are his feet”), one sees them with his physical eyes, and it is hard to make them look like leaves again. One is not seeing more than before; it is the same picture. So G-d hides, and this book shows us where the bunnies are. [▶ 15:13](listen:021#913)

One then says, “Here is the hand of G-d”: *chesed* the right arm, *gevura* the left. When the Jews went out of Egypt, the magicians saw the finger of G-d, and later the hand of G-d was seen. They did not see any hand; they saw a thing that was the hand of G-d. [▶ 18:03](listen:021#1083)

**For, since he grasps the essence of this concept very well, he is, therefore, capable of bringing it into many separate matters.** It is not enough to understand what the book explains; one must go over it a few more times, until it is absorbed. [▶ 18:46](listen:021#1126)

## §20

**For example, [he can understand] many details in the existence of the created worlds, how they come into being from nothing to something**, details that were **concealed** in the general comprehension he had before, like the bunnies in the picture. [▶ 19:10](listen:021#1150)
