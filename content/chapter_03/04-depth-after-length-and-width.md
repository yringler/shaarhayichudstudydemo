## §11–12

The brief summary just described is the shortness that comes before. [▶ 4:53](listen:021#293) In contrast, **the way this concept is grasped to its essential depth after the abundant explanations and comparative analogies, is called the depth and essence of this concept**. [▶ 4:59](listen:021#299) It looks the same as the brief version, but it really is not. [▶ 5:09](listen:021#309)

> **Key point:** The depth after all the length and width can look like the short summary, yet the two are not the same.

**It is specifically after the length and width of the [many] analogies and abundant analysis that he will come to the aspect of the depth that is understood through them.** [▶ 5:17](listen:021#317) The Yiddish in the text says it: to perceive the real depth of how the thing truly is. [▶ 5:31](listen:021#331) With this the author has recapped chapter one in a single passage. [▶ 5:49](listen:021#349)

## §13

So far he has explained *bina* and applied it to *memalei kol almin*. [▶ 5:54](listen:021#354) *Bina* in it means going through the concept with all its particulars, all the different analogies and all the different explanations, whether of spreading forth or of revelation; that is the width. The length is bringing each one down into an analogy: for instance, the revelation of the hidden is like the letters of speech revealing the letters of thought, or it is the analogy of a river. The length makes it physical, bringing it down into physical terms. [▶ 6:01](listen:021#361)

**Everything that was previously explained regarding the particulars of *Keter, Chochmah* and *Binah* of *Binah*** refers to chapter one: *bina* of *bina* is *hisbonenus*; *chachma* of *bina* is discovering a new thing within the same thing; *Keser* is the *omek hamusag*, and the deeper *Keser* is the *shaar hanun* (the fiftieth gate). [▶ 6:51](listen:021#411) All of it **may be found in a particular concept such as this**: in any particular comprehension throughout the book, the whole matter of *bina* will be found, as in *memalei kol almin*, **and likewise, in every other particular concept in the comprehension of *HaShem*’s-יהו״ה G-dliness, as will be explained** later in the book. [▶ 7:10](listen:021#430)
