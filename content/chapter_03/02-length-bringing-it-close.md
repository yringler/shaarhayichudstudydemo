## §4–5

**We previously explained that the aspect of *Iyun* - analysis is the aspect of the vessel which brings one to the aspect of the depth.** [▶ 1:50](listen:021#110) **The length is the aspect of bringing the concept close to one’s intellect through the use of the various known analogies.** [▶ 2:00](listen:021#120) This is the *orech* (length) of *bina*; the *iyun* (analysis) is what leads to its depth. Here the author is recapping what he has already said, so the lines are largely self-explanatory. [▶ 2:06](listen:021#126)

The length goes on **until the matter becomes very close to his mind**: in the Yiddish of the text, he has brought the intellect very close to himself. [▶ 2:21](listen:021#141) It is done **by investing it in physical [analogies]**, since the analogies are a physical garment, **to the point that even a child can grasp it**. [▶ 2:32](listen:021#152)
