## §1

**Now, it is known that in *Binah* itself there are two levels; *Binah* and *Tvunah*.** [▶ 0:15](listen:018#15) *Bina* (understanding), as discussed in the first chapter, basically means analysis. **The matter of *Tvunah* is the aspect of the spreading forth of the light of *Binah*.** [▶ 0:23](listen:018#23) The definition continues after the parenthesis, in the next line: the light spreads forth because the concept is grasped well in the receiving vessel. The parenthesis brings the Zohar, where *tevuna* is **called the lower mother** (*Ima tata'a*).

## §2

The light of *bina* spreads forth because the concept **is grasped very well in the vessel of the comprehension of *Binah***. [▶ 1:03](listen:018#63) This too comes specifically from *hisbonenus* (contemplation), from going over the matter again and again. [▶ 1:06](listen:018#66) When one contemplates a matter until it is understood well and stops there, he has *bina*, but not *tevuna*. If he keeps going over it even after he understands it well, it is absorbed into the receiving vessel of the comprehension, into his brain. Then **it spreads forth to bring it into many matters that are separate from the essential comprehension**: it can go out into various things that are separate from the understanding itself. [▶ 1:12](listen:018#72)

One who can do this is called **“a man of *Tvunah*,”** the one who can **draw them out**, the deep waters. [▶ 1:57](listen:018#117)

> **Key point:** Understanding a matter well gives *bina*. *Kelita* (absorption), and with it *tevuna*, comes from going over it again after it is already understood.

## §3

**The “deep waters” represent the aspect of the depths of *Binah* which remain concealed in the essential self.** [▶ 2:14](listen:018#134) The real depth of a concept stays concealed within the concept itself, as with the matters studied in this very book. [▶ 2:22](listen:018#142)

This book is always a *mashal* (analogy) on itself. Here the Mitteler Rebbe is the man of *tevuna*: specifically he **draws it out and brings it forth from its concealment, to bring it to spread forth in many different manners, in order to quench the thirst of the recipients**, and the recipients are us. [▶ 2:38](listen:018#158)
