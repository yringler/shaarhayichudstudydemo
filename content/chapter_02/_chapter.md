## Overview

The first chapter dealt with *bina*, the analysis and understanding of a concept. This chapter shows that *bina* has two levels: *bina* itself and *tevuna*, the light of *bina* spreading forth. *Tevuna* comes from *kelita*, absorbing a matter by going over it again even after it is understood. The man of *bina* goes down into the deep well and drinks there; the man of *tevuna* brings the water up to the sheep. Without *tevuna* a person can understand a Talmudic debate perfectly and still be unable to rule on it, and whole societies can think great thoughts that never come to anything. *Tevuna* is the lower mother: it turns the emotions within *bina* into real emotions of the heart, through *daas*. *Hisbonenus*, *bina sheb'bina*, joins the two, and that is why contemplation is a service of G-d and not only an intellectual exercise.
