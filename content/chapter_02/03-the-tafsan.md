## §7

**This is not the case with one who is not “a man of *Tvunah*.”** [▶ 11:02](listen:018#662) He may be **a man of comprehension in his mind of *Binah*** (a *tafsan*, a “grabber,” who has a good grip on the matter): a person with *bina*, but not so much *tevuna*. Then **it is possible that his comprehension remains as it is in the essence of his mind of *Binah***: he understands it, but his understanding stays just the way it is. He can drink the water because he went down into the depth. [▶ 11:25](listen:018#685) Yet **there will not come from it any spreading down into separate matters, outside of the essential comprehension itself**: no application, and no explanation to anyone else. [▶ 11:58](listen:018#718)

> **Mashal:** Societies show this too: the Greeks and the Americans. The Greek power was *bina*; the American, and the whole modern Western, power is specifically *tevuna*: not so much understanders as appliers. [▶ 12:07](listen:018#727) The Greeks had a working steam turbine, knew about electricity, the wheel, metal, and oil from the ground that burns, and could have started the Industrial Revolution two thousand years ago. For them it was all pure intellect, the pursuit of intellect and not of the dollar, and they never thought of it on so low a level as application. [▶ 12:34](listen:018#754)

So a person, and even a whole society, can stay entirely within *bina* with no spreading forth from it at all, even though he may be a greater master of *bina* than the man of *tevuna*. [▶ 16:34](listen:018#994) A Greek philosopher would have considered the Wright brothers or Edison crude people (*grobbe mentshen*), thinking about how to build a better horse carriage. Nonetheless a person can have more *tevuna* even though he does not have so much *bina*. And the advances of *tevuna* also come specifically from *bina sheb'bina* (bina within bina), from the analysis of the thing. [▶ 17:44](listen:018#1064)

## §8

Furthermore, **although his comprehension is in the length, width and depth, as explained above, it is all in the essential comprehension, as it is.** [▶ 18:04](listen:018#1084) Even his explaining it “downward” is really to himself, not to others; to explain to somebody else is specifically the work of *tevuna*. [▶ 18:12](listen:018#1092) However, **it will not be revealed below in separate matters**, and revealing a thing in a separate matter is just what a *mashal* does. [▶ 18:26](listen:018#1106)

All the more, **the depth of the concept remains in its depth, concealed in itself**, as with the Greek philosophers: nothing came out of it. Not only that, they did not even explain it to ordinary people. It was for a few of the elite, who were happy to think about these things and never thought to explain them to the man working in the marketplace. In America, by contrast, the whole idea is that everybody has to learn it and understand it. [▶ 18:38](listen:018#1118) **Likewise, the length and width all [remain] in the essential comprehension, as they are, in that state alone.** [▶ 19:33](listen:018#1173)
