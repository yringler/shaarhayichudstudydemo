## §21–22

**For this reason, *Tvunah* is also called *Ben U’Bat* – Son and Daughter, as known.** [▶ 20:37](listen:019#1237) The letters of תבונה, rearranged, spell בן ובת. *Ben u'vas* means *chesed* and *gevura*, that is, *daas*: as explained later, the mind of *daas* divides into two *mochin*, *chesed* and *gevura*. [▶ 20:55](listen:019#1255) So *tevuna* is where *daas* comes from; bringing the matter into an actual feeling of *middos* comes specifically from *tevuna*. It is the actual mother of the children, the lower mother. *Bina* does not bring the children directly, only through the lower mother. [▶ 21:12](listen:019#1272)

**This is because *Tvunah* likewise possesses the three [dimensions of] depth, length and width. Its depth is the essential strength of the power of *Tvunah* of this comprehension, as it is during the time which he grasps it**: the actual *kelita*. [▶ 21:39](listen:019#1299) *Kelita* takes the thing in by absorption, not only grabbing it and holding on. Holding on is from the outside; *kelita* is absorbing it like a sponge, so that it becomes part of oneself. [▶ 21:52](listen:019#1312)

**Through this (grasp) he is capable of bringing the [very] depths of the concept [out] into revelation**: specifically through this *kelita*, the depth of the concept comes out, as in the verse **“a man of *Tvunah* shall draw them (the deep waters) out.”** [▶ 22:15](listen:019#1335)

## §23

**The length of *Tvunah* is the descent [of the concept] into a separate matter, to shed light upon it, or to accomplish many actions through it**: bringing it down into a separate thing, either to teach and explain it to somebody else, or to apply it in actual action. [▶ 22:32](listen:019#1352)

## §24

**Its width is the aspect of the spreading forth of his *Tvunah* in the essential comprehension [itself], to expand it in his thought in many different manners**. [▶ 22:56](listen:019#1376) This means the width of *bina* really comes from the width of *tevuna*: only with *kelita* can there be real expansion within *bina* itself. [▶ 23:07](listen:019#1387)

He adds one more thing, mentioned before though not here: since *bina* has a height, so does *tevuna*. From the power of *kelita* one comes to understand one thing from another, and only one who has absorbed the matter can do so. That is why he is called a *navon*: the root of *navon* is *tevuna*, not *mevin*. A *navon* is a master of *tevuna*. [▶ 23:29](listen:019#1409)

## §25

The reason is that **it is specifically *Tvunah* that gives birth to revealed emotions as matters in and of themselves**, *chesed* and *gevura*, through the mind of *daas*. [▶ 24:06](listen:019#1446) Yet **from *Binah* itself, there is no birth of the emotions**, because *bina* remains entirely intellectual: there is always another hand, another analysis. [▶ 24:22](listen:019#1462) This is so **although *Binah* is called the “mother of the children,”** since without the original *hisbonenus* there would be no *tevuna* either. [▶ 24:34](listen:019#1474)

*Bina* is **the “upper mother”**: in a way, the grandmother, the original source but not the immediate one. [▶ 24:43](listen:019#1483) It could be called the *savta* (grandmother), as *Yisroel Saba* is called the grandfather. *Bina* is not the mother of the *middos*; it is the mother of *tevuna*, the mother of the mother of the *middos*. That is, it is the mother of its own *middos*, and those *middos* of *bina*, when they come out, are *tevuna*. [▶ 25:05](listen:019#1505)

> **Key point:** *Bina* is the mother of *tevuna*; *tevuna* is the mother of the emotions.

## §26

**In contrast, *Tvunah* is called the “lower mother,”** which gives birth to the *middos*: **love and fear, which specifically come about included in *Tvunah*.** [▶ 25:55](listen:019#1555) Only in *tevuna* do all the “other hands” come together in *hiskalelus* (inclusion) to bring out the actual, final *midda*. In *bina* it is all still in little pieces, a piece here and a piece there; putting the pieces together into an actual product happens only in *tevuna*. [▶ 26:04](listen:019#1564)

It is **the aspect of the spreading forth of the light of *Binah* downward**. At the start of the chapter he did not say “downward”; now he defines it more precisely: what is generally called *tevuna* means specifically downward, the lower mother. [▶ 26:40](listen:019#1600) The real power of *tevuna*, spreading, goes in other directions too, but those have other names: *chachma* of *bina*, *bina* of *chachma*, and so on. And just as *chachma* and *bina* have a source in the hidden *chachma* and *bina* of *Arich*, *tevuna* also has a source in the hidden *tevuna* of *Arich*: since *bina* includes both *bina* and *tevuna*, so does the *bina* of *Arich*. [▶ 27:01](listen:019#1621)
