## §20

**This then is the aspect of the lower father and mother, *Yisroel Sabba* and *Tvunah*. *Yisroel Sabba* is the aspect of the emotions of *Chochmah*, and *Tvunah* is the aspect of the emotions of *Binah*.** [▶ 14:20](listen:019#860) Yet they are not simply the *middos* of *chachma* and of *bina*: **these two are how these emotions come into revelation, to the outside.** [▶ 14:28](listen:019#868) As just explained, the *middos* of *bina* are not *tevuna*; one can have them and be unable to apply them. The *middos* of *bina* as they stay inside are just that; *tevuna* is those *middos* as they come out of *bina*. It is the *middos*, not the intellect of *bina*: the *Chabad* of *bina*, its actual *hisbonenus*, stays entirely within itself. [▶ 14:41](listen:019#881) Even so, though *Yisroel Saba* and *Tevuna* are only the *middos* of *chachma* and *bina*, each becomes a full *partzuf* (configuration) of ten *sefiros*, so *tevuna* too has its own *Chabad*. [▶ 15:22](listen:019#922)

**The aspect of *Malchut* of *Tvunah* is the aspect of the intellect which comes in thought, which is called the letters of intellectual thought (*Machshevet Sechel*)**: thought as it still is within the intellect. Later it will be explained as *Leah*. [▶ 15:39](listen:019#939) **It is the external vessel for analytical thought (*Machshava Iyunit*)**. Analytical thought is the actual *iyun* (analysis) taking place inside the intellect, and it is clothed in the *machsheves sechel*, the actual letters. The distinction is very fine: there is no analytical thought without the external letters. The letters come specifically from *Malchus* of *tevuna*, because it takes the inner analysis and puts it into a separate thing: letters, words and concepts. [▶ 16:14](listen:019#974)

One might ask: is *Malchus* of *tevuna* then not *Leah* itself, only its root? It is still part of the essence of the intellect, as it is coming out. *Leah*, in terms of the worlds, is already like *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*: actual thought, speech and action. Thought is no longer part of the essence; it is a *levush* (garment). *Malchus* of *tevuna* is still part of the body, the actual inner intellect. [▶ 17:19](listen:019#1039)

> **Mashal:** Right now we are talking. The letters of speech are a wholly separate garment, nothing but sounds: over the telephone they are turned into pulses of electricity, then back into movements of air, then into signals along the listener's nerves. My speech came from my thought, which is letters too and not really part of me, only a more inward garment, in my brain, put on and taken off. *Malchus* of *tevuna* is still part of the brain itself; *Leah* is already like talking inside one's head. [▶ 18:12](listen:019#1092)

All this is explained much later in the book; here it is only introduced. *Malchus* of *tevuna* **is the aspect of the large *Dalet* (ד) of the word *Echad*-אחד**. [▶ 20:06](listen:019#1206)
