## §27–28

**It is also for this reason that the aspect of the intellect of the emotions is drawn from the aspect of *Tvunah*.** [▶ 27:40](listen:019#1660) The *mochin* (intellect) of the *middos*, the *mochin* of *Zeir Anpin*, come from *tevuna*, not from *bina*. [▶ 27:48](listen:019#1668) As the verse says, G-d founded the earth with *chachma*: “**the father founded the daughter**,” *Malchus* comes from *chachma*. [▶ 27:56](listen:019#1676)

“**He established the heavens with *Tvunah***”: with *tevuna*, not with *bina*. [▶ 28:09](listen:019#1689) As known, **the “heavens-Shamayim-שמים” refers to the aspect of the emotions**, fire and water, *chesed* and *gevura*. [▶ 28:13](listen:019#1693)

## §29

**This refers to the establishment of the emotions [because] of the aspect of the intellect of *Tvunah* which is in them**: the *mochin* of the *middos* come specifically from *tevuna*, **since the aspect of *Tvunah* is like an intermediary to bring the intellect from the aspect of the emotions of *Binah* into the emotions of the heart.** [▶ 28:35](listen:019#1715) The *mochin* of the *middos* come from the *middos* of the *mochin*, the *middos* of *bina*; but to become the *mochin* of the *middos*, they must pass through *tevuna*. [▶ 28:55](listen:019#1735)

This is what is called the *uvchein*, the “and therefore.” [▶ 29:23](listen:019#1763) The *mochin* of the *middos* are an *uvchein*: not the essence of the intellect, the *Chabad* of *bina*, only the *middos* of *bina*. That is why in *avodas halev* (the service of the heart), when the matter is brought down into the heart, only its end passes through, the *uvchein*, and not the intellect itself; whereas in *avodas hamochin* (the service of the mind) the whole *Chabad* of *bina* comes into it. [▶ 29:39](listen:019#1779)

## §30

**This is similar to the power to bring [a concept] into a separate matter, which is the principal aspect of *Tvunah***. [▶ 30:01](listen:019#1801) Here it means bringing the intellect into a *midda*, since a *midda* is something separate from the intellect. The power of *tevuna* does several things: to teach others, and to apply; and applying includes bringing out the actual *middos*, bringing the intellect into the application of an actual feeling. [▶ 30:19](listen:019#1819)

From the recap at the start of the next class: the main point of *hisbonenus* is to bring it into the *middos*. That was the whole point of *Kuntres HaHispaalus*: not just to be a *maskil* (one who understands) but an *oved* (one who serves). *Hisbonenus* is *avoda*, not *haskala*, an intellectual exercise. Many people make a big mistake in thinking that those who learn something like *Shaar HaYichud* are *maskilim* with their heads in heaven. [▶ 0:12](listen:020#12) The *hisbonenus* is specifically *tevuna*, because *daas* comes from *tevuna*: *ben u'vas*, the mind of *daas*, which brings about actual *middos*. [▶ 1:04](listen:020#64)

As the author writes there, what his father the Alter Rebbe wanted was for Chassidim to have *hispaalus Elokus*. The goal is the *middos*: to get excited, not to sit back and say, “How interesting, so what is *Arich* and what is *Atik*?” [▶ 1:38](listen:020#98) The goal of *avodas halev* is to get fired up. A greater goal is *avodas hamochin*, where the whole mind is fired up and the thought of *bina* itself grows hot. Greater still, up in *Atik* and *Arich*, the *ratzon* (will) and *taanug* (delight) grow hot, so that this is where one's delight comes from. [▶ 2:20](listen:020#140)

Higher than that is the essence of the *yechida*: “this is what I am.” It is said of the Mitteler Rebbe that if he were pricked with a needle, Chassidus would come out instead of blood, the blood being the soul. [▶ 2:59](listen:020#179) This is above delight: one who wants something for its delight wants it only while the delight comes. The essence of the soul is willing to give up everything, even its life *al kiddush Hashem*, as even a simple Jew is; and why? Because this is what I really am. These are the five levels of *hispaalus* that he explains there. [▶ 3:40](listen:020#220)
