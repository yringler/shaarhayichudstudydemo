## §15–16

We see that **in *Binah* itself, there exists an aspect of emotions, which are called the seven lower *sefirot* of *Binah*.** [▶ 4:32](listen:019#272) What came before was the *Chabad* (intellect) of *bina*; now he speaks of the seven lower levels of *bina*, the *middos* of *bina*, which are the forty-nine gates. **These are [also] called the emotions of the intellect that leans either towards merit, or the intellect that leans towards guilt**. [▶ 4:39](listen:019#279) When one analyzes something, besides understanding it one also comes to a view that it is a good thing or a bad thing. In studying Chassidus, for example, the analysis leads to the decision that this is good. That is an intellect leaning toward merit, and it is already a *midda*, not the actual understanding of what Chassidus is. [▶ 5:03](listen:019#303) That decision is what will awaken the *daas*, for the thing or against it. [▶ 5:33](listen:019#333)

However, **these are only as the emotions still are concealed within the essential comprehension.** [▶ 5:41](listen:019#341) It is not an actual feeling of liking something; it is a mental decision that this seems good and ought to be liked. The *middos* of the intellect are not *middos*; they are intellect. [▶ 5:48](listen:019#348)

## §17

Nonetheless, **to bring these emotions out into revelation in a separate matter**, **to [actually] cause the judgment on this matter to lean either to kindness or sternness, specifically requires a man of *Tvunah*.** [▶ 6:12](listen:019#372)

> **Mashal:** In *bina* there are forty-nine *middos*. It is like the man in *Fiddler on the Roof*: on the one hand it is a good thing, but on the other hand, from this *sevara*, it is bad, and on the other hand, from that one, it is good. As long as it is in the intellectual brain of *bina*, there are many other hands. [▶ 6:33](listen:019#393)

But to come out with what it finally is: that is already *tevuna*; to arrive at a *psak halacha* (halachic ruling) takes specifically a man of *tevuna*. [▶ 7:19](listen:019#439) Later he explains that the real ruling comes from *daas*, the mind of *hata'a* (inclining): *bina* says this and that, and finally one says, “I have to decide already.” [▶ 7:35](listen:019#455) That is why someone who thinks too much cannot be a good businessman: a time comes when he must decide without knowing all the details, and in *bina* there is always another detail; details are infinite. [▶ 8:19](listen:019#499) Where the reasons all weigh to one side (should I jump from an airplane without a parachute?) no inclining is needed. It is needed where the balance is close and some things cannot be known now. [▶ 9:38](listen:019#578)

What makes a great authority like the Rambam or the Rif is that they learned the matter so well that they had *kelita*: not only did they understand it, they absorbed it, and it became part of their brain. Therefore they can arrive at a halachic decision usually out of *tevuna*, without having to resort to a feeling for it. [▶ 10:52](listen:019#652) The basic work of *tevuna* is explanation and application: before, explanation, bringing the water out to the sheep; now, application, whether in halacha or in science, as with the Greeks' steam engine. [▶ 12:34](listen:019#754)

## §18–19

**The meaning of “a man of *Tvunah*” is that he has a thorough grasp of the essential comprehension**: the absorption of the comprehension. [▶ 13:12](listen:019#792) **Because of this, the comprehension spreads forth in him, to do and to shed light upon many completely separate matters.** *Kelita* is the *Keser* (crown) of *tevuna*. It too comes from *hisbonenus*, the double *nun*, going over and over. If one goes over the matter until it is understood and stops there, there is no *kelita*. [▶ 13:19](listen:019#799)

**He even is capable of bringing the depth of *Binah* itself into revelation**. [▶ 13:40](listen:019#820) So there are two things, application and explanation. The essence of *tevuna* is *kelita*, and its power is to spread the matter out. Generally, “*tevuna*” means downward: either down into application, or down into explanation to somebody else. [▶ 13:56](listen:019#836)
