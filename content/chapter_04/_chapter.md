## Overview

Chapter 4 asks how contemplation should be done: as a whole (*derech klal*) or detail by detail (*derech prat*). The general way holds the real goal, that G-d is in everything and everything is nullified to Him. But on its own it lets a person believe he understands while G-d stays far away, and can even lead to false delusions. Particulars bring the idea close. Starting from what can be grasped, often from how things work in ourselves, one climbs step by step: from the orbits and angels to *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, and through the *partzufim* to the *Kav*, until the general is reached truly. Each unification is a detail of a higher one; the general unification is the nullification of everything to G-d's essence. The particulars are studied only to reach Him. In prayer, too, the particular meanings strengthen the general intent, though a beginner starts with the general.
