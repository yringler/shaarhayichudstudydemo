## §46–47

**Through all of the above we have answered the question of those who inquire regarding the specific [kabbalistic] meanings of the words**: **How is it that those who know them do not become confused [and distracted] from the general intent?** [▶ 16:12](listen:023#972)

The same question is asked about this study, though it is study and not yet prayer with the meanings of the words. Many people ask: what are you studying? The main thing is that there is nothing besides Him. You are just intellectuals interested in all kinds of kabbalistic matters, who want to know all sorts of particulars. [▶ 16:22](listen:023#982) The answer is no, and this is what the *Mitteler Rebbe* tells us his father told him, and the *Maggid* told his father: it does not confuse one's understanding of G-dliness. [▶ 16:51](listen:023#1011)

**Rather, on the contrary, the intent of the meaning of the words [of prayer], as explained by Kabbalah, serves to strengthen the depth of one’s general grasp of the Essential Self** of G-d. [▶ 17:13](listen:023#1033) "Why learn kabbalistic matters? The main thing is that there is a G-d and nothing besides Him": this is one of the big complaints against our way. But it is **known to those who have tasted** this learning in a particular way. [▶ 17:24](listen:023#1044)

## §48

So how should one pray: should one start praying according to the intentions of the *Arizal*? No. [▶ 17:57](listen:023#1077) Rather, **for those who are novices, it is best that, at first, they should delve in a general way, until they become accustomed, little by little**, to the particulars. When praying, they should simply pray as one standing before the *Aibershter*, until they have learned more, and then they will be able to pray this way too. [▶ 18:04](listen:023#1084)

In connection with this he mentions *lechatchila ariber* (going straight over the top, from the outset). [▶ 18:25](listen:023#1105) First comes learning, then prayer: first the learning of the matters and their particulars, then depth of *daas* in them, to bring the intention to the Essence in prayer, which is the service of the heart. [▶ 20:01](listen:023#1201) The *Rebbe Maharash*'s *lechatchila ariber* meant the service of the mind; the *Rebbe* said a second *lechatchila ariber* on top of it, "depth within depth". [▶ 20:29](listen:023#1229) The *Rebbe* said we are already in the days of *Moshiach*, and explained the *Rambam*'s words that then the whole world's occupation will be only to know G-d. Then even a man polishing his car in the corner, which seems not just a particular unrelated to the general but its very opposite, a *kelipa*, is nothing but "there is nothing besides Him", understood properly. That is not the far-off pantheistic nonsense that the world is G-d. [▶ 20:55](listen:023#1255)

So this book, which used to be called *Kuntres HaHisbonenus* (the tract on contemplation), is for us *Shaar HaYichud* (the gate of unity); if it is to be called a tract, it should be called a tract on knowing G-d. [▶ 23:13](listen:023#1393)
