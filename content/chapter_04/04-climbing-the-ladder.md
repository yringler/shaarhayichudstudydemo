## §15

The example of particular contemplation in *memalei kol almin* starts with **how the root of the spiritual influence of the orbits and constellations come into actuality, out of nothing, from the waste matter of the** *Ofanim* angels. [▶ 15:56](listen:022#956)

This runs backwards from the way the book works. The book's explanation is top-down; the contemplation is bottom-up. Afterwards, when one thinks the matter over, one starts from the part one understands. [▶ 16:12](listen:022#972) And even when the explanation runs top-down, it is understood as it is down here.

## §16–18

Once he understands how the physical world comes out from the *Ofanim*, he goes on to the *Ofanim* and the *Chayos*, the angels, until he reaches **the aspect of *Malchut* [of the world] of *Asiyah,* which is the general G-dly light that brings the world of *Asiyah* into being**. [▶ 16:48](listen:022#1008)

From *Malchus* of *Asiya* he goes up through **the details of the ten *sefirot* [of the world] of *Asiyah*** to *Keser* of *Asiya*, and from there to **the aspect of *Malchut* of *Yetzirah*, which becomes the *Keter* [for the world] of *Asiyah***. [▶ 17:25](listen:022#1045)

**In this manner** he goes up through *Yetzira* and then *Beriya*, like Jacob's ladder. [▶ 17:46](listen:022#1066) This is also what prayer (*tefilla*) is: contemplation is divine service (*avoda*), and *avoda* is *tefilla*, and *tefilla* means going up the ladder.

## §19

He goes up to *Keser* of *Beriya*, and so understands how *Malchus* of *Atzilus* becomes *Keser* of *Beriya*: *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, **the totality of everything** that includes all of *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*. That is *yichuda tata'a*. [▶ 18:09](listen:022#1089)

This is not the explanation, which goes from above to below; it is about seeing, as in "Lift up your eyes on high." [▶ 18:30](listen:022#1110) Once one has the explanation in particulars, one can literally look at the world.

> **Mashal:** It is cold today because it is winter, and it is winter because of where we are in the constellations as the orbits turn. That is why the leaves are falling and why I have to wear a sweater. My heater is on, and the gas bill will hit me at the end of the month: the orbits are spending my money on gas. If it affects me, it affects the man next door, all of Pittsburgh, all of North America, the price of gas, the price of energy, even the situation of Israel and the Arabs. One goes up from level to level through the particulars, and what moves all of it is the orbits, the constellation we are under. [▶ 18:43](listen:022#1123)

From there one goes up and up: from the orbits to the *Ofanim*, from the *Ofanim* to *Malchus* of *Asiya*, all the way to *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, which includes all the outer worlds, **and is generally called by the name G-d-*Elohi”m*** (*Elokim*). [▶ 21:18](listen:022#1278)

## §20–21

Then the matter truly comes **in a way of great closeness**: G-d is behind my high gas bill. It is the name *Elokim*, not coincidence. [▶ 21:39](listen:022#1299) The good food on *Shabbos* came from the sun, the moon, the water and the earth, and those were affected by the years: one year a drought, another a frost, and oranges were expensive. Everybody says the *Aibershter* gives us oranges; this is following the trail of exactly how He gives them. I bought the orange in a store, but the orbits affect how much money I have and what oranges cost. The angels affect the orbits, and *Malchus* of *Asiya* affects the angels. Like a private investigator, one follows the trail up and finds who did it: the *Aibershter*, *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, *Elokim*. [▶ 22:26](listen:022#1346) Then nobody can spin a tale and persuade me that there is no G-d: I can show the whole trail of how G-d got me the orange. [▶ 23:37](listen:022#1417)

Yet **each particular of his contemplation is generally not a matter of G-dliness**. It may have nothing to do with G-dliness at all, like the sweater one started with. It is **only a particular that is of secondary importance**: why even talk about it? We want to talk about G-d, and you are talking about your sweater and your gas bill. [▶ 23:55](listen:022#1435)

> **Mashal:** Nonetheless, **when his whole analysis of all the particulars, comes together and becomes bound to the general principle**, you have an airplane. Everybody knows what an airplane is, but the engineer knows where every screw goes and all its aerodynamics. "It flies because it has wings"? Make a thing with wings and throw it: it falls. In engineering school there is the screw class, the propeller class, the algebra class, and the student asks what algebra has to do with airplanes. A child who wants to be a doctor is first taught A, B, C. First all the particulars, then one can actually be a doctor. [▶ 24:48](listen:022#1488)

So although the real thing is specifically the general, only when one knows all the particulars does one really know the general. [▶ 27:03](listen:022#1623) **This has been tried and tested by all those who have toiled deeply in** contemplation: by anyone who has done the deepest contemplation properly. [▶ 27:25](listen:022#1645)
