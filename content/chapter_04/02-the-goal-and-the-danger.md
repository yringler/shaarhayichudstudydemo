## §9–10

In truth, **the two [methods] are dissimilar**: one is not really like the other. [▶ 4:20](listen:022#260) On the one hand, **there is an advantage to general contemplation**, since through it one reaches **the general aspect of the essence of the G-dly light**. The real thing is, in fact, specifically the general. [▶ 4:41](listen:022#281)

It is the same as in chapter 1: why go for *bina* (understanding) when the real thing is *chachma* (wisdom)? *Chachma* is the general (*klal*) and *bina* is the particular (*prat*). Here too, what one is really after is the main thing, the *klal*: in *yichuda tata'a*, how G-d fills the world, and in *yichuda ila'a*, how the *sefiros* are unified with the Emanator. That is what one really wants. [▶ 5:08](listen:022#308)

One does not need every detail, such as how the letters come out of *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *tevuna* after beginning in analytic thought. When one gets into the nitty-gritty of analogies like that, it can seem that one is not talking about G-dliness at all, only about how language is made, how the mind cuts an idea up into letters. [▶ 5:26](listen:022#326) The general way speaks of the main thing: how G-d is everywhere and in everything, and how everything is nullified (*batel*) to the *Aibershter*. **This revelation of G-dliness in his soul is the ultimate purpose of contemplation.** The general is the real goal. [▶ 6:32](listen:022#392)

## §11–12

On the other hand, **there is also an advantage to contemplating specifically in a way of particulars**: it brings the revelation of the light much closer to his soul. [▶ 7:01](listen:022#421) Why? Because in general contemplation **it is possible for one to delude himself**, so that he imagines he really understands.

**However, in truth, when he only [contemplates] in a general manner**, G-d is seen from afar. [▶ 7:24](listen:022#444) One who says "G-d is all, all is everything and all is one" has only a very vague idea. He can take that general notion and understand it in a way that is exactly the opposite of how it really is, while thinking that he understands it well. This is because it is only general, and he does not know the actual particulars of how it is so. [▶ 8:00](listen:022#480)
