## §28

**“Yours, *HaShem*-יהו״ה, is the greatness.”** With this the text begins to explain the particular unification (*yichud prati*) and the general unification (*yichud klali*). [▶ 2:52](listen:023#172) The matter of G-dly unification is the depth of comprehending the nullification of the "something" to the "nothing", in both the upper and the lower unity.

A particular unification is the unification of greatness: for instance, how *Malchus* of *Asiya* is connected to *Malchus* of *Yetzira*, *Malchus* of *Yetzira* to *Malchus* of *Beriya*, and so on up to *Malchus* of *Ein Sof*. [▶ 3:06](listen:023#186) But that is only a particular unification. Beyond it, one connects all the particular unifications to G-d Himself: the nullification of the "something" of the particulars to the total "nothing", understanding the whole thing together. [▶ 3:29](listen:023#209)

## §29–30

**Now there [also] is a particular unification for each particular thing.** In “Yours, G-d, is the greatness,” greatness is the aspect of *chesed* of *Malchus* of *Atzilus* in *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*, **in a particular way for each world**: understanding how the *chesed* of *Atzilus* comes into the kindnesses of each particular world. That is only a unification of the kindnesses. [▶ 4:08](listen:023#248)

**That is, even the general matter here is only a single particular quality**. Even the whole of it is only a particular, and treating it as the whole is still a mistake, like the people who say "G-d is love." Then why do so many bad things happen? What happened to justice? [▶ 4:25](listen:023#265) They follow one particular. If one knows only the matters of kindness, the right line and not the left, one cannot put them together and go the next step higher. [▶ 4:49](listen:023#289)

## §31–32

The same is true of might: **“Yours *HaShem*-יהו״ה is the might,” both in general and in its particulars in each world, [it] is, [nonetheless], considered to be a particular unification**. [▶ 5:24](listen:023#324)

**The same [principle] applies to the unifications of all the particulars of the ten *sefirot*** of *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*. One learns all the particulars, then the unifications of the particulars, until one can unify all the *sefiros* of the upper worlds with the same *sefiros* in the lower worlds. [▶ 5:28](listen:023#328)

## §33–34

**In contrast, a general unification is like [the understanding of]** "Behold, the place is with Me": the whole “place” of the world is with Him. It means understanding that the world is nothing but G-dliness, truly understanding what that means, not in the idolatrous sense. [▶ 5:46](listen:023#346) “**The exalted King**”: the *Aibershter* is King of the whole world, **aloof** from the six directions and from *chachma* and *bina*. The *Aibershter* Himself is higher than all ten *sefiros*, even the ten essential *sefiros*. [▶ 6:10](listen:023#370)

Compared to the essence of *Malchus*, **even this is, [likewise], only considered to be a particular.** Here the text speaks of the ten *sefiros* of *Malchus* of *Atzilus*: understanding how the King is entirely above its separate *sefiros*. [▶ 6:40](listen:023#400) Even that is only a particular compared to the inner part of *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, which is *Keser* of *Malchus*. [▶ 7:09](listen:023#429)

## §35–36

In the same way one goes higher and higher through the ten *sefiros* of *Atzilus*, **until the essence of the aspect of *Malchut* of *Ein Sof* (the Unlimited One), at the beginning of the *Kav***, unifying everything into it. [▶ 7:26](listen:023#446) **There are many unifications of the many particulars in each *sefirah* and *partzuf***, and each unification is a detail compared to a higher general idea. Even a great unification is still only a detail compared to the next level up. [▶ 7:37](listen:023#457)

Finally, after this whole ascent, there is the general unification, which **is the general nullification of the entire chaining down of the *Kav,* relative to the Essential Self of the Limitless Light**. [▶ 8:09](listen:023#489)
