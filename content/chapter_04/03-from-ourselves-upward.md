## §13

**This is not the case when one contemplates in a manner of particulars**, thinking about particular ideas through their various explanations. [▶ 8:08](listen:022#488) Then **it becomes embedded in his soul in a very close way**: it comes into his will, which becomes bound up with his mind.

From such a particular, for example *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *tevuna*, or how *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya* comes down into the *kelipos*, he can understand a great deal. [▶ 8:43](listen:022#523) This is not a grasp of what the thing is in itself (*mahus*), but an actual grasp of its existence (*metzius*): exactly how these things exist. And from that he can understand the level above.

What was really understood in the first three chapters, about *chachma*, *bina* and *tevuna*, with analogies such as the difference between the Americans and the Greeks, was how we ourselves have *bina*, *tevuna* and *chachma*. Strictly, it was not even the *Chabad* of the *kelipos* in general but of our own *kelipa*, our own physical nature. [▶ 9:52](listen:022#592) Yet from that particular one goes to the general: from our own *bina* to the general *bina* of the *kelipos*, from there to the *bina* of the spiritual worlds of *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya*, and from there up to *Atzilus* and higher.

> **Key point:** The whole of "His understanding is unsearchable" was not grasped, but our own *tevuna* was grasped truly: so well that it can be explained exactly to someone else, with new analogies made to fit his kind of mind. That is unlike the one who says "all is one and one is all" and does not know how it works. [▶ 11:13](listen:022#673)

It can seem that this is not about G-dliness at all, only how the human mind works: we came to understand G-dliness and are learning psychology. That is why the text said that the two methods are not alike (*lo re'i zeh*). [▶ 12:46](listen:022#766) But from this, "from my flesh I perceive G-d," one understands a higher general idea, which in turn becomes a particular of a still higher one, and so on. [▶ 13:09](listen:022#789)

## §14

**From this, he will come to comprehend a [higher] particular**, step by step, **until he comes to the general comprehension of the general matter**: how it is in the *Chabad* of the *Or Ein Sof*. [▶ 13:23](listen:022#803) Of course, what that is in itself remains unknown. If one truly knew it, one could do the same and create a world. But it does not matter: what one understands is the existence of the relationship between these things. [▶ 13:34](listen:022#814)

Take delight and will (*taanug* and *ratzon*): there is no delight without will and no will without delight. This is understood from oneself: I want something because I enjoy it, and I enjoy it because I want it. [▶ 14:10](listen:022#850) Why does a person want a steak? Because he enjoys it. Why does he enjoy it? Because he is hungry and wants it. The same holds for more refined things, such as honour or an idea: going up the ladder from a physical steak to intellect, will and delight are one.

From this one can understand the relationship of will and delight all the way up to the will and delight of G-d's essence, which cannot itself be grasped, being part of the Essence, entirely undefined. Even so, one understands that there is no will without delight and no delight without will: the relationship is truly understood. [▶ 15:12](listen:022#912) In this way **[the comprehension] is truer, without deluding himself at all**, and he does not make the mistake described earlier. [▶ 15:39](listen:022#939)
