## §1–2

**After having explained the need to contemplate specifically in a manner of particulars, it follows, of necessity, that one must know all the particular levels** in all the details of *seder hishtalshelus* (the chain of descending worlds), in order to contemplate them. [▶ 0:44](listen:060#44)

**These particulars cannot be understood by everyone through the general teachings**. All the details have to be known, and they are not available anywhere else. [▶ 0:59](listen:060#59) "The general teachings" means works like *Etz Chaim*. Certainly not from the *Zohar* or *Sefer Yetzira*, and even from the works of the *Arizal* it is not possible to understand what they are talking about. So what is the solution? [▶ 1:17](listen:060#77)

## §3

Therefore, **whatever needs explanation in each detail, will be explained in a special pamphlet**. [▶ 1:41](listen:060#101)

> **Mashal:** When reading the *Megilla*, at "this second letter" one shakes the scroll, to show that this is the letter being spoken of. So at "a special pamphlet" one should shake this book: this is the book he means. [▶ 1:43](listen:060#103)

It is a promise: everything is explained in this book. Not literally every detail, but what is needed from each particular, and once that is understood well, **a person of understanding will automatically understand** the other particulars as well. [▶ 2:19](listen:060#139) That is a very big promise: to explain all of *Kabbala* and *Chassidus* in this book, with every particular needed to do what chapter 4 asked. [▶ 2:45](listen:060#165)

## §4

Before that, **it first is necessary to introduce one more point** about **how *Hitbonenut* contemplation should be done**. [▶ 3:04](listen:060#184) The book has said what this contemplation is, what it is about (*seder hishtalshelus*), and that it must be done in particulars. Now it explains the how: there is a certain way it has to be done. [▶ 3:14](listen:060#194)

This is **extremely necessary for all those who truly desire closeness to** G-d **and do not seek to merely fulfill their obligations and deceive themselves**: for whoever wants to study properly without fooling himself, and not just to have done his duty: "I learned a *sicha* today." [▶ 3:33](listen:060#213)
