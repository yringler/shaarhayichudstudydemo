## Overview

Every contemplation of creation has two sides. One is the "something": how a created thing comes from "nothing". The "hearing" of *bina* can grasp this fully, and grasping it brings felt joy. The other is the G-dly "nothing" itself, which cannot be grasped. It is glimpsed only in the "sight" of *chachma*, a flash that is as certain as seeing with one's eyes, and it brings *bittul*, a loss of all sense of self. The deeper one goes, the more one understands, and the farther above the essence appears. It works this through for the orbits, which come from a mere glimmer of *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya*, and for *Atzilus*. Its great principle is that the joy and the *bittul* must come together, in exact proportion. Comprehension without *bittul*, or *bittul* without comprehension, is self-deception, and real humility is not lowliness.
