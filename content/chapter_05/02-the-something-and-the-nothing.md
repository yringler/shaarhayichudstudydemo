## §5–6

As is known, **every contemplation upon the G-dly matter of the coming into being of something from nothing**, any contemplation at all, wherever one learns *Chassidus*, **includes two matters**. [▶ 3:51](listen:060#231)

**The first is the aspect of the nullification of the “something” to the “nothing.”** It **begins with the comprehension of the relativity of limited beings, by understanding how their existence is derived from the “nothingness.”** [▶ 4:11](listen:060#251)

## §7

**This is brought close to the intellect through various explanations and analogies and the like**: how the "something" comes out of the "nothing" can be fully understood through explanations, images and analogies (*meshalim*). [▶ 4:26](listen:060#266) The text adds, in Yiddish (a line the translation leaves out), that it lies very well in the mind: this is something that can be explained until it really settles well in one's mind. [▶ 4:51](listen:060#291)

## §8–9

**The second matter is the aspect of the G-dly “nothingness” that bring him into being**, and how G-d is separate beyond comparison, **since He has no limitations**. [▶ 5:00](listen:060#300) When learning how something comes into being, there is also the question of where it comes from: the G-dly "nothing" (*ayin*), which is entirely infinite. And "infinite" does not mean what most people think, like an infinite number. It means entirely undefined. [▶ 5:09](listen:060#309)

**This matter is concealed from the eyes of the intellect and cannot be fully grasped**: it cannot be understood with a full comprehension (*hasaga*). [▶ 5:34](listen:060#334)

## §10

**However, the truth of it will, nonetheless, flash like lightning in his brain**. A flash is a matter of *chachma*, while "full comprehension" is a matter of *bina*. [▶ 5:42](listen:060#342) At the same time as the comprehension, there should be a flash of what it is really talking about. Although the explanation cannot tell you what the thing truly is, there should be a flash of *chachma* into what it really is, **as it is**. [▶ 6:03](listen:060#363)

## §11

However, **although it is in an aspect of concealment, it will be as true for him as if he saw it with physical sight.** Seeing with the mind is like seeing with the eyes. [▶ 6:22](listen:060#382)

> **Mashal:** You could explain everything about an airplane, its aerodynamics and its mathematics, to someone who never heard of one, until he understands that it could work. That is how the Wright brothers worked: there had never been an airplane, but from aerodynamics, and from seeing that birds fly, they figured one could be made to work. Then there is someone to whom nothing was explained, but who saw an airplane fly. Someone who has all the explanations can be argued out of it with better explanations that it is impossible. Someone who saw it fly cannot be convinced that there is no such thing. [▶ 6:45](listen:060#405)

So the flash, though it is concealed, is not an explanation, and does not include the particulars, is in a way a stronger certainty than the explanation. [▶ 8:04](listen:060#484)
