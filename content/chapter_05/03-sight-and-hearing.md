## §12

**This is the difference between the aspect of the “sight” of *Chochmah*** and the “hearing” of *Bina*. The sight of *chachma*, the power of "what" in the soul, **is the sight of the soul that sees, but not with tangible sight**: he sees with his mind's eye. [▶ 8:28](listen:060#508) Rather, **it is a flash of intellectual sight which is beyond full comprehension**: he sees a flash of the thing, not by chopping it up into an explanation. [▶ 8:39](listen:060#519)

## §13

**Therefore, the arousal that results from this is nothing but the aspect of nullification** (*bittul*) toward the idea. [▶ 8:49](listen:060#529) It is **the complete absence of awareness of self**: the basic emotion that comes from *chachma* is that he does not feel himself. [▶ 9:03](listen:060#543)

The text adds in Yiddish (a line the translation leaves out) that he is moved from his place, and does not feel even this. [▶ 9:19](listen:060#559) He does not even feel that he is not feeling. Why? Because when he has the flash, all he feels is the flash. He does not feel himself, and does not even notice that he does not feel himself. That is the sign of the flash of *chachma*. [▶ 9:37](listen:060#577)

## §14

**The aspect of the “hearing” of *Binah*, on the other hand, comes with full comprehension, literally.** [▶ 10:06](listen:060#606) The grasp of the analogies and the material is a real *hasaga*. *Hasaga* is related to *masig gevul*, one who moves the fence into his neighbour's property: he actually goes into the other man's property. So *hasaga* means that the mind actually enters the domain of the thing. [▶ 10:20](listen:060#620)

What it grasps is **how the “something” comes into existence from the “nothing,”**: for instance, how the worlds of *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya* come out of *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, and also *yichuda ila'a* (the higher unity). [▶ 10:42](listen:060#642)

## §15–16

**The resulting arousal is a tangible feeling of joy and good-heartedness.** It is the exact opposite of the arousal from *chachma*: not that he does not feel himself, but the reverse; he feels himself more and more. [▶ 11:01](listen:060#661)

**The more he grasps, the better he feels**: the more he understands, the happier he is. [▶ 11:18](listen:060#678) The text puts it in Yiddish: it is very good and revives the soul, a really good thing for him. "I am getting such great insights, I am growing and growing with this." He actually feels that it is good for him and that he is becoming bigger. [▶ 11:24](listen:060#684) And **the more he delves into it, the more he grasps**. [▶ 11:48](listen:060#708)

## §17

**In contrast, the aspect of the “sight” of *Chochmah* is the opposite. The more he delves into it**, the farther away it is. [▶ 12:01](listen:060#721)

> **Mashal:** Someone who knows no *Chassidus* at all, when he says "the *Aibershter*", thinks we live on the first floor and the *Aibershter* lives on the second. Someone who learns a little more says He lives on the tenth floor; then it becomes the Empire State Building; then it is in the clouds; then all the way up to the moon. Each time it is farther away. Those who have not learned *Chassidus* almost think of the *Aibershter* as a physical thing, a spiritual-physical thing made of air: we are made of lead and He is made of helium. The more *Chassidus* one learns, the more one sees that it is totally beyond. [▶ 12:15](listen:060#735)

And what is totally beyond is even *Malchus* of *Asiya*, never mind higher levels. [▶ 13:14](listen:060#794) The more he delves, the more **the G-dly “nothingness” that creates, becomes removed and astounding to him**: what it actually is (*mahus*), not its existence (*metzius*). Existence means knowing what the relationships are; what it really is goes farther and farther away. [▶ 13:30](listen:060#810)

## §18

**This astonishment over the awesome gap and absence of any comparison** grows and grows, until it makes him truly sad and he worries that it is going farther and farther away: How will I ever understand this at all? How will I have a connection to it? **(as it is written in the Chassidic manuscripts)**. [▶ 13:56](listen:060#836)
