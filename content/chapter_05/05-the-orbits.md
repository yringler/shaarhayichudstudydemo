## §21–22

Take **one who contemplates in a manner of particulars into the coming into being of the planetary orbits from the G-dly “nothing,”**, which comes **from the aspect of the external vessels of *Malchut* of *Malchut* of *Asiyah***. [▶ 17:26](listen:060#1046) The text deliberately chooses the lowest things, something one can actually see: one looks around and sees the sun, the moon and the stars going around, and the earth, a big ball, going around too. [▶ 17:34](listen:060#1054) And he considers how this comes out of the outer vessels of *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya*: **merely a glimmer of a glimmer, which radiates within the soul of the celestial orb** as it grasps what creates it. [▶ 17:55](listen:060#1075)

## §23

**Likewise, he understands how the angels of the world of *Briyah*** grasp **the G-dly power of *Malchut* of *Malchut* of *Atzilut* which brings them into existence**. [▶ 18:04](listen:060#1084)

This adds something. Everybody says there are *yichuda ila'a* and *yichuda tata'a*, the higher and the lower unity. But within *yichuda tata'a* there is another: this physical world comes out of *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya*. Then *Beriya*, *Yetzira* and *Asiya* come out of *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Atzilus*, and *Atzilus* comes out of *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Ein Sof*. [▶ 18:31](listen:060#1111)

## §24

**He grasps these concepts in a way of comprehension, and he becomes aroused by the greatness of the Creator, and how they [the angels, stars and planets] are constantly sublimated [to Him]**, as the verse says, "And the hosts of the heavens bow down to You." [▶ 19:03](listen:060#1143) The universe is so vast, and each star is like a gigantic atomic explosion, yet all the power of the whole universe is nothing compared to *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya*. [▶ 19:20](listen:060#1160)

## §25–26

**However, the aspect of the G-dly “nothingness,” the essential creative force of *Malchut* of *Asiyah*, is concealed from his grasp.** What is *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya* really? [▶ 19:43](listen:060#1183) We understand the relationship of the world to it, its existence (*metzius*); what it actually is (*mahus*) is hidden from comprehension. [▶ 19:51](listen:060#1191)

**However, it is automatically forced into his comprehension**: not that he actually understands what it is, but the mind's logic dictates that it must be so. [▶ 20:04](listen:060#1204) The universe is so big and so strong. The sun is a gigantic atomic explosion with an amazing amount of power, and the sun is a tiny dot in the whole universe; and moving all those stars around takes fantastic amounts of power. All of that comes from *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya*, so the mind is compelled to say: what an amazing thing *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya* must be. But he does not actually understand what it is: it is *muchrach b'hasaga*, forced on the mind. [▶ 20:24](listen:060#1224)

This comes **because the understanding of how the “something” comes into existence has become true for him**: once it is clear to him how the "something" of the orbits comes into being from *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya*, and not even from all of it, only from the edge of its chambers, he says: Wow, what it must be! If this is only the little bit that comes out of it, imagine what it is! [▶ 21:19](listen:060#1279)

## §27

Furthermore, **it is only comprehended in a concealed way**: only as something the mind is forced to conclude, not that he actually gets it. [▶ 22:05](listen:060#1325) And it is grasped **because of the astonishment over the total absence of comparison**, in sheer amazement. [▶ 22:14](listen:060#1334)

> **Key point:** Understanding how the orbits work, how this one goes around that one, gives him joy. Thinking about what *Malchus* of *Malchus* of *Asiya* actually is gives him a "wow", a wonder (*hafla'a*): "I can't even imagine what it must be." Not "imagine what it is", but the very response is that one cannot imagine it; it is entirely removed from him. [▶ 22:32](listen:060#1352)
